Wrait turns your voice into a private, searchable diary. Just speak — AI transcription turns your words into organized text entries. Your thoughts stay encrypted on your device, never uploaded to the cloud.

KEY FEATURES

• Voice-to-Text Diary: Record your thoughts and get instant AI transcription powered by Deepgram and OpenAI. Your spoken words become clean, readable diary entries.

• Strong Local Encryption: Every entry is encrypted with SQLCipher before it touches storage. Your encryption key is generated on your device and never leaves it.

• 60+ Languages: Record and transcribe in over 60 languages including English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Russian, and many more. Cloud mode detects your language automatically.

• App Lock: Protect your journal with fingerprint, face unlock, or your device PIN. No one reads your diary without your permission.

• Export Your Data: Download all your entries as CSV. Your data belongs to you.

• Clean, Modern Design: Built with Material Design for a smooth, native Android experience. No clutter, no ads.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Tap the button and speak your thoughts
2. AI transcribes your voice to text automatically
3. Review, edit, and save your encrypted diary entry

No account required. No sign-up. Just open and start talking.

YOUR PRIVACY COMES FIRST

Unlike other diary apps, Wrait was built for privacy from the ground up:
• All entries are encrypted before being stored
• Your encryption key is generated on-device and never shared
• No cloud backup — your diary exists only on your phone
• Screenshot protection prevents screen captures of your entries
